Report sentence

Method: IC = VT / (VT/IC). IRV = IC − VT. A fall in serial IC is interpreted as an equal rise in end-expiratory lung volume when TLC remains stable and the IC maneuvers are adequate. Because the source report rounds VT/IC to a whole percentage (±0.5) and VT to 0.01 L (±0.005), each IC lies within a plausible range, IC = VT / (VT/IC ± 0.5%). The range for a change from rest combines the resting and exercise ranges conservatively. The resting maneuver dominates the uncertainty because its VT/IC is smallest.

Operational interpretation: Dynamic hyperinflation is labeled clear only when the entire plausible range of the IC fall meets both thresholds (≥0.15 L and ≥10% from rest); borderline when the point estimate meets at least one threshold but the range does not clear both, or when the point estimate meets neither but the range cannot exclude both; and not clear otherwise. Inspiratory mechanical constraint is labeled marked when calculated IRV is ≤0.5 L, and approaching when VT/IC is ≥60% or IRV is ≤1.0 L. VT/IC alone does not trigger "marked" because a large-lunged patient can reach 70% with ample IRV. These are practical descriptors, not universal diagnostic cutoffs. Review the flow-volume loops, maneuver quality, breathing pattern, spirometry, and the complete CPET.

References: O'Donnell DE, et al. Ann Am Thorac Soc. 2017;14(Suppl 1):S30-S39. DOI. Examples of published operational thresholds include Collins SE, et al. Am J Respir Crit Care Med. 2025;211:1519-1522. DOI, and Huber dos Santos A, et al. Respir Med. 2026;260:108924. DOI.
