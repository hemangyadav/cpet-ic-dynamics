Report sentence

Method: IC = VT / (VT/IC). IRV = IC - VT. A fall in serial IC is interpreted as an equal rise in end-expiratory lung volume when TLC remains stable.

Operational interpretation: Because IC is reconstructed from whole-number VT/IC values, the app uses a conservative three-level flag: clear requires both an IC fall of at least 0.15 L and at least 10% from rest, while meeting only one threshold is labeled borderline. Inspiratory mechanical constraint is labeled marked when VT/IC is at least 70% or IRV is at most 0.5 L, and approaching when VT/IC is 60% to 69% or IRV is 0.51 to 1.0 L. These are practical descriptors, not universal diagnostic cutoffs.

References: O'Donnell DE, et al. Ann Am Thorac Soc. 2017;14(Suppl 1):S30-S39. DOI. Examples of published operational thresholds include Collins SE, et al. Am J Respir Crit Care Med. 2025;211:1519-1522. DOI, and Huber dos Santos A, et al. Respir Med. 2026;260:108924. DOI.
