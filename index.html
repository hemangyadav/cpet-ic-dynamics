Report sentence

IC = VT ÷ (VT/IC); IRV = IC − VT. A fall in serial IC from rest is read as an equal rise in EELV, assuming TLC is stable. Dynamic hyperinflation: IC fall from rest ≥0.15 L (a conventional operational threshold). Critical inspiratory constraint: VT/IC ≥70% or IRV ≤0.5 L; approaching: IRV ≤1.0 L.

O'Donnell DE, Elbehairy AF, Webb KA, Neder JA. Ann Am Thorac Soc. 2017;14(Suppl 1):S30–S39. DOI. O'Donnell DE, Guenette JA, Maltais F, Webb KA. Chest. 2012;141(3):753–762. DOI. Guenette JA, et al. Pulm Med. 2013;2013:956081. DOI.
